Thoughts & insights

Leadership
Leading a tech startup at a young age
I founded Archon Tech while still a first-year AI & ML student. There's no manual for running client calls between lectures. What works: treat every client like your biggest one, say no to scope you can't deliver well, and keep building in public — most of my early clients found me through people watching the process, not a pitch.
SEO
Zero-budget SEO for small businesses
Most small business owners think SEO means a big monthly retainer. It doesn't. Start with a complete Google Business Profile, a fast mobile-friendly site, and the words your customers actually search — not the terms in your brochure. That's an afternoon of work, not a budget line.
Web development
Building modern responsive websites in 2026
Responsive used to mean "does it work on mobile." Now it means the site should feel considered on every screen. The shift isn't more breakpoints — it's content that adapts: type with real hierarchy, layouts that reflow, images that stay sharp without slowing the page down.
